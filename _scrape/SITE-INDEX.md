## Accommodation Types

1. **Camping** — tent/motorhome pitches
2. **Chalet Hütte** — 4-bedroom house, 10-12 persons, €260-295/night
3. **Tiny Häuser** — wooden cabins, 4-5 persons, €105-115/night
4. **Mobilheime** — mobile homes, up to 6 persons, €105-125/night
5. **Kleine Holzhütten** — log cabins, 4 persons, €75-85/night
6. **Wohnwagen** — caravans, 2-3 persons, €55/night
